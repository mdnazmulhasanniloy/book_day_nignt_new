Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Beverly Group Srl (“BookDia Night”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use our mobile apps, APIs, and related services.

Effective: 1 November 2026 Controller: Beverly Group Srl Roma, Italy

1. Who we are

BookDia Night is operated by Beverly Group Srl, Via Sant’Anselmo, 34, 00153 Roma, Italy. Contact: support@bookdianight.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email, phone number, profile details you provide.
  • Booking data — club/event selections, guest counts, attendees, quotes, guest-list entries.
  • Payment data — amounts, currency, Stripe payment references (card details are processed by Stripe, not stored by us in full).
  • Device data — device identifier, platform, approximate location you grant, FCM push tokens.
  • Usage data — app interactions needed to operate bookings, notifications, and support.

3. Why we use your data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process reservations, tickets, VIP modules, waiting lists, and guest lists
  • Send transactional notifications (booking confirmed, ticket ready, guest-list decisions)
  • Prevent fraud, secure payments, and meet legal obligations
  • Improve product reliability and customer support

4. Legal bases

Depending on the activity, we rely on contract performance, legitimate interests (secure operations and product improvement), consent (where required, e.g. certain notifications or location), and legal obligation.

5. Sharing

We share data only as needed with:

  • Venue owners / organizers — to fulfill your booking or guest-list request
  • Payment processors — Stripe for checkout and payouts
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting, storage, email/push delivery
  • Authorities — when legally required

6. Retention

We keep account and booking records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and satisfy accounting or legal requirements, then delete or anonymize when no longer necessary.

7. Your rights

Where applicable under GDPR and similar laws, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing. Contact support@bookdianight.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encrypted transport, access controls, and least-privilege operational practices. No method of transmission is 100% secure; please protect your login credentials.

9. Children

BookDia Night is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
