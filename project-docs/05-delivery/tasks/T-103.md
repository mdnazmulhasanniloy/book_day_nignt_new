## Steps

1. `POST /v2/clubs/{clubId}/team` (owner only): invite by email with a role and assigned events; stores a hashed single-use token and emails the invitation. `GET` lists members.
2. `POST /v2/team/invitations/{token}/accept`: the invited user (existing or newly registered customer) accepts; token expires after 7 days and works once.
3. `PATCH` (role, assigned events, suspend) and `DELETE` by the owner; platform `PATCH /v2/admin/team-members/{id}/suspend` for any account (reason required).
4. Every change writes a `PermissionAuditLog` row.
