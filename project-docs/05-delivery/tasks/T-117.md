## Steps

1. When a hold is unsupported or expired, after venue acceptance send the customer a payment link/intent for the exact accepted amount ('fresh payment'); the table stays reserved for the accepted-table hold window (separate clock, default 24 h, configurable by the club).
2. Re-check availability at payment time; if the table was sold elsewhere the quote is withdrawn path (T-113) and the customer pays nothing.
3. Payment success confirms the booking exactly as T-116.
