## Steps

1. Add per-table policy: deposit refundable or non-refundable, optional refundable terms text and deadline, set by the club; shown before payment.
2. On acceptance store a snapshot of the terms the guest accepted (text, version, time, user). The booking applies those terms; the venue may grant exceptions through the refund engine (club exception).
