## Steps

1. Implement `POST /v2/admin/partner-debits/{id}/waive` (Admin only): waive part or all of the platform margin, down to the documented unrecovered Stripe cost; approver and reason logged.
2. Implement `POST /v2/admin/charge-to-club` (Admin only) for chargebacks caused by an event cancellation or change; record the customer refund, the platform contribution and the remaining debit separately.
