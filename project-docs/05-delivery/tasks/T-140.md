## Steps

1. On a venue or organizer cancellation refund the admission and reverse its Service Fee. Refund the customer Technical Fee only where the per-country setting `refundTechnicalFeeOnPartnerCancel` requires it (the default comes from the adviser).
2. Calculate the partner debit: the full Technical Fee actually refunded, shown with original fee, actual processor cost, waiver and debit before approval.
