## Steps

1. Replaces the superseded T-078 (approve or reject requests). Add "Refunds oversight": list from `GET /v2/admin/refunds` with status, actor, amounts; detail shows original Technical Fee, actual processor cost, waiver and the partner debit before approval.
2. Admin-only actions: waive part or all of the margin (never below the unrecovered Stripe cost) and "Charge to club" for chargebacks, each with a required reason; the approver is logged by the server.
