## Steps

1. With test keys and two test connected accounts: create a charge on the platform, then a transfer to a connected account later; reverse it fully and partially; try a transfer larger than the available balance; simulate a failed payout (invalid IBAN test number) and a missing-document restriction.
2. Measure when charged funds become available for transfer per country, and how advance sales (event weeks away) behave: holding limits, reserve, and how the platform keeps enough available balance.
3. Write `05-delivery/reports/stripe-spike-connect-<date>.md`: sequence diagrams, error codes for failed transfers, retry rules, where funds sit when a partner account is restricted and whether sales can continue.
4. Demonstrate (script) one partially refunded order with a transfer reversal and one insufficient partner balance.
