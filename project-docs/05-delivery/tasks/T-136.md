## Steps

1. Implement `POST /v2/bookings/{id}/tickets/{ticketId}/cancel` for the customer: allowed until the published cutoff and never after a scan; invalidates only that ticket's credential and keeps the others.
2. `verify-ticket` (v1) and the v2 scan refuse a cancelled ticket (422 TICKET_NOT_VALID).
