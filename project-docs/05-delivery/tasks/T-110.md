## Steps

1. Add additive fields `teamRole` (null for owners and customers), `clubIds` and `permissions` (flat list of granted keys) to the login, check and refresh responses of members and platform staff. Existing fields and `accountRole` values are untouched.
2. Staff members sign in with the same endpoints as customers; the app reads `teamRole` to show role-based navigation.
