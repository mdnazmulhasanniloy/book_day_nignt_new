## Steps

1. Add "Settlements": per event and partner, the states Pending, Confirmed - scheduled for transfer (with date), Transferred, Failed; shows held amounts for open refunds or disputes.
2. Retry a failed transfer with `POST /v2/admin/transfers/{id}/retry` (confirm dialog). Link to the partner statement.
