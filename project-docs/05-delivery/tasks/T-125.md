## Steps

1. When the organizer has no payments-enabled Connect account, route the organizer share to the eligible club and show it in the preview (`fallbackApplied: true`).
2. When the club cannot receive funds either, set the arrangement to NEEDS_RESOLUTION, block sales and notify the owner and admin.
3. For approved unregistered venues the organizer receives the seller proceeds (no club share).
