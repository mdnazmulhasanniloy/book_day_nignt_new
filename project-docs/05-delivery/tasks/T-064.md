## Steps

1. Grep every `order.refund` / `refund:` condition in `server/src` and `worker/src` (verify-ticket, owner dashboard, earnings, bookings status, ticket PDF recovery, notifications) and list them in the PR.
2. `POST /owner/verify-ticket` rejects only when the order has a refund with `refundType = FULL` (422 `TICKET_NOT_VALID`, as T-011). A PARTIAL refund leaves entry valid.
3. Owner dashboard and earnings: a PARTIAL refund reduces net revenue by the refunded amount but does not mark the order as refunded or canceled; a FULL refund behaves as today.
4. `bookingStatus` (T-046): REFUNDED only for FULL refunds; a partial refund shows CONFIRMED with a refunded-amount field if the spec has one.
