## Steps

1. `GET /v2/admin/venue-authorizations` (status filter, paging) and `POST /v2/admin/venue-authorizations/{id}/decision` with `decision` APPROVED or REJECTED and a required note for REJECTED.
2. Only roles with the venue-review permission (Admin, and platform staff or country managers within their country) may decide; others get 403 (admin client only).
3. Notify the organizer (in-app and push) on each decision; log approver and reason.
