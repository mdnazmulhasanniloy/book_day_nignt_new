## Steps

1. Replace plain `Error` throws with errors that carry `statusCode` and `errorType`: not found OR not your order: 404 (do not reveal that another seller's order exists); not PAID or refunded: 422 `TICKET_NOT_VALID`; already used: 409 `TICKET_ALREADY_USED`.
2. Do not use 401 or 403 anywhere in this handler (the Flutter client maps both to 'unauthorized').
3. Keep one check-in per order (`Order.checkedInAt`) exactly as today. Do not touch the refund check yet: the `refundType = FULL` rule is T-064.
4. Keep the human messages the app shows; add `errorType` additively.
