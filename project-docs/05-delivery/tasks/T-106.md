## Steps

1. `PUT /v2/owner/organizers/{organizerId}/permissions` (owner): sets quotes, chat, refunds independently; all three are OFF until switched on (Q24 d).
2. Each change writes a `PermissionGrant` row (grantedBy, grantedAt, revokedAt) and an audit row; history is readable by the owner and admin.
3. The permission is scoped per club (and optionally per event) and checked server-side in the quote, chat and refund services through `resolvePermissions`.
