## Steps

1. With Stripe TEST keys (never live): create a PaymentIntent with `capture_method=manual`, confirm with test cards, capture, cancel, and let one expire (use the shortest available `capture_before`). Record the exact `capture_before` returned per card type.
2. Check which payment methods in IT, FR, ES, US support manual capture (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, SEPA, wallets) and what happens with 3D Secure on authorization and on capture.
3. Test the Connect case: authorization on the platform account with later transfer; and the failure paths (capture after expiry, insufficient funds on capture, declined re-auth).
4. Write `05-delivery/reports/stripe-spike-hold-<date>.md` with the results, authorization costs under the client's contract and a recommendation for ADR-005 (hold where possible, fresh payment otherwise).
