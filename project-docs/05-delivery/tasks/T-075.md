## Update 2026-11-24 (client answers 2)

Phase 2. The rule form changes with the client rates (answers 2026-11-24, Q1 and Q2, ADR-003 amended):
- A FEE rule can be a percentage AND a fixed part (default Technical Fee: 5% of the eligible paid subtotal plus EUR 0.50, local-currency amount per currency, fixed part charged once per order). A COMMISSION rule (Service Fee) is 10% for tickets and passes and 15% for tables, sunbeds and extras.
- New scopes in the form: country, venue, individual table or quote override (in addition to global, club and event); precedence shown as country and category default, then partner or club, then event, then table or quote.
- Display mode per market and category (itemized or included) and the optional partner fixed charge for free admissions.
- Show the saved effective date and never edit an active rule (existing behaviour).
Wording: the client calls the customer booking fee "Technical Fee" and the partner commission "Service Fee". Use those labels in new text (en and it). API field names are not renamed.
Permissions: the page and its actions follow the role-permissions model (ADR-008). Hide or disable actions the signed-in role cannot use, and show the server message on 403. A Country Manager only sees rows of their country. Platform Staff cannot see rates, payouts or roles.
