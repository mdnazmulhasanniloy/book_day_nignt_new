## Steps

1. Recover a `PartnerDebit` in order: eligible partner balance, then future settlements, then an invoice record for the shortfall. Never charge the same cost twice (unique key per cost).
2. The customer's refund is independent of recovery.

## Done when

- [ ] Test: a balance that covers the debit creates no invoice.
- [ ] Test: a partial balance recovers what it can, then the next settlement is reduced, then an invoice record is created for the rest.
- [ ] Test: running recovery twice does not charge twice.
- [ ] Test: the customer refund is paid even when recovery fails.
