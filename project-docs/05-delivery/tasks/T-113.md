## Steps

1. Customer `POST /v2/table-requests` (authenticated); the club, or an organizer with the quotes permission (T-106), issues `POST /v2/table-requests/{id}/quote` with price, inclusions, expiry (default 48 h, configurable, never beyond the booking deadline) and cancellation policy.
2. `/withdraw-quote` by the club while unpaid, with reason; the customer is notified and payment is disabled on a withdrawn quote.
3. An expiry job (worker, delayed job at `expiresAt`) moves unpaid quotes to EXPIRED; a quote is 'subject to availability until paid' and says so in the response `disclosure` text.
4. T-056's 409 QUOTE_REQUIRED on `POST /purchase/module` remains and its message points to this flow.
