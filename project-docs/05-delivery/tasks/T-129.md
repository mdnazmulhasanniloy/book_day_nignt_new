## Steps

1. On a transient transfer failure retry up to 3 times over 3 days with the same idempotency key.
2. On an invalid IBAN, missing document or restricted account notify admin and partner immediately with the required action; no retry.
3. After the third failure notify admin and partner; the money stays on the platform balance and sales continue.
