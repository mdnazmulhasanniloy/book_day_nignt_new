## Steps

1. Implement `POST /v2/owner/orders/{orderId}/refunds` for club exceptions after the cutoff or on non-refundable bookings, and for documented partial goodwill refunds with admission kept (reason required).
2. A goodwill refund never invalidates the ticket. For Italian events the goodwill path is off until the adviser confirms the fiscal treatment (setting by market).
