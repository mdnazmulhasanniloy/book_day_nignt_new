## Steps

1. On each new message detect phone numbers, e-mail addresses and off-platform contact words (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, "pay me directly") and create a `ChatFlag`; do not block the message.
2. The POST response carries the additive `protectionNotice` key ("For your protection, please keep this booking in the app.").
3. Do not flag the approved on-site balance workflow: when the booking payment plan is "deposit online, balance at venue", talk of paying the balance at the venue is not flagged.
