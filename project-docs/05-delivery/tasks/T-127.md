## Steps

1. Add a scheduler job that enqueues one worker job per due `Transfer` (CONFIRMED entries whose expected date has passed). Withdrawal stays disabled before release.
2. The worker creates the Stripe transfer from the platform balance with an idempotency key built from the ledger entries and stores the result on `Transfer`. Subject to Stripe availability: an insufficient available balance leaves the transfer SCHEDULED and records the reason.
