## Steps

1. `GET /explore`, `/explore/feed` and `/explore/map` accept a `cityId` that is an area: match events whose `geog` is inside the boundary or within the area members; the 10-50 km radius is ignored for areas.
2. `GET /cities` gains additive `kind` (CITY or AREA) and `areaId`; add `/v2/admin/destination-areas` GET and POST.
3. Add an additive `emptyReason` (NO_CLUB_IN_CITY or NO_RESULTS) to list responses with `total` 0, so the app can show the right text.
