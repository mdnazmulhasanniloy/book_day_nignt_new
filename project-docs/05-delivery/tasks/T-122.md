## Steps

1. When the online amount is smaller than the Service Fee on the full price (e.g. only EUR 200 paid online against EUR 150 commission), allocate the commission from the online amount where settlement permits and record the remainder as a `PartnerReceivable` row (amount, reason, booking, status OPEN).
2. The receivable is visible to admin and the partner and feeds the recovery order of the refund engine (T-141).
