## Steps

1. Extend `PromoCode` (additive): purpose DISCOUNT or GUEST_LIST_REQUEST, event or list, expiry, use limit. A limit of 1 is single-use and 100 is shared.
2. A guest-list code submits a guest-list request; the ticket is issued after approval where required.
