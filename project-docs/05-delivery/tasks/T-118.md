## Steps

1. The club selects which modes it offers per table: DEPOSIT_PLUS_VENUE, FULL_ONLINE, TWO_ONLINE; the customer chooses among those offered; amounts due now and later are shown before acceptance.
2. Fees: percent Technical Fee on every in-app amount, fixed part once on the first online payment, nothing on the venue balance. Service Fee 15% on the full base price in all modes.
3. Record attendance and both payment channels on the booking (online payments and a VENUE_BALANCE record confirmed by the venue).
