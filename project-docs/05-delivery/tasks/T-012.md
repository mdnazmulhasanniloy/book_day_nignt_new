## Steps

1. Add one small helper `mapPrismaError(error)` in `src/app/utils/` that converts `P2025` to 404 and FK violation `P2003` to 409 (or 404 when the missing parent is the cause, document the rule), keeping everything else as is. Unit-test it with fake `PrismaClientKnownRequestError` objects.
2. Use it in the club and event services for `GET /club/:id`, `PUT /club/:id`, `GET /event/:id`, `PUT /event/:id`. Not found and not owned both return 404.
3. Do not touch eventModule, promo or wishlist here (T-013, T-018).
