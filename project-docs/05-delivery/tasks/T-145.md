## Steps

1. Reconcile Stripe refunds, disputes and bank credits against refunds, partner debits and platform contributions per event; a credit that repays a customer already refunded is matched, never paid twice.
2. Produce a reconciliation read for admin: per event totals of customer refunds, contributions, debits and recoveries.
