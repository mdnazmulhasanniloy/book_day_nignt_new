## Steps

1. Before a transfer: reverse the affected internal shares, including the applicable Service Fee, on refund.
2. After a transfer: create a full or partial Stripe transfer reversal; record any unrecovered amount as a debit candidate for the partner debit task (rf_debit).
3. Never reverse more than was transferred.

## Done when

- [ ] Test: a refund before transfer reduces the ledger shares and no reversal call is made.
- [ ] Test: a partial refund after transfer calls one partial reversal for exactly the partner's share.
- [ ] Test: an insufficient partner balance records the unrecovered amount and a debit candidate.
- [ ] Test: a reversal retried with the same key is applied once.
