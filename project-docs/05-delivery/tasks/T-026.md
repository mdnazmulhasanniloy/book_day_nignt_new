## Steps

1. Allow a tier price of 0 only when the organizer sets it explicitly on that tier (opt-in): `pricingMode` FREE or MIXED. A zero price anywhere else stays rejected as today.
2. In `POST /purchase/event` for a total of 0: skip Stripe, create the Order as PAID with `serviceChargeAmount = 0` and `commissionAmount = 0` (provisional until Q2), create the purchase and attendees, increment `soldCount` and generate the ticket as for a paid order. Respond with `freeCheckout: true` and no client secret.
3. Idempotency (`Idempotency-Key`) and per-user limits apply as for paid orders. Promo codes on a free ticket are ignored.
4. Why this is in M3 and not M2: a free order needs the relaxed `Order` CHECK from migration 6 (T-022).
